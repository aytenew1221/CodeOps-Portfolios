About Habesha Market

Habesha Market is a local marketplace located in Kirkos, Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. We offer a wide variety of authentic Ethiopian products, including Ethiopian coffee beans, teff flour, berbere spice, shiro powder, fresh injera, organic honey, traditional Habesha Kemis, Mesob baskets, Jebena coffee pots, and fresh fruits and vegetables. Our mission is to provide high-quality products while supporting Ethiopian farmers, artisans, and local businesses. We are committed to serving our customers with fresh products, fair prices, and excellent customer service.
