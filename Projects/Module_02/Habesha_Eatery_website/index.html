Our Story

Welcome to Habesha Eatery

Habesha Eatery brings the rich flavors and traditions of Ethiopian cuisine to Bole, Addis Ababa. Our dishes are prepared with carefully selected ingredients and traditional spices.

From freshly baked injera to flavorful wot and tibs, every meal is prepared to give our guests an authentic Ethiopian dining experience.
